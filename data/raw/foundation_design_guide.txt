FOUNDATION DESIGN GUIDELINES - TURKISH BUILDING CODE

SECTION 1: FOUNDATION REQUIREMENTS

General Requirements:
- All structures must be founded on competent soil or rock
- Minimum foundation depth is 1.0 meter, but generally 1.5-3.0 meters recommended
- Foundation depth must be below frost line (varies by region, typically 0.8-1.2m in Turkey)
- Bearing capacity must be verified through site investigation

SECTION 2: BEARING CAPACITY CRITERIA

Allowable Bearing Pressures (typical values):
- Dense sand and gravel: 300-500 kPa
- Medium dense sand: 150-300 kPa
- Loose sand: 75-150 kPa
- Stiff clay: 150-300 kPa
- Medium clay: 100-150 kPa
- Soft clay: 50-100 kPa
- Rock (sound): 500-2500 kPa

For buildings, apply safety factor of 3.0 minimum.

SECTION 3: SETTLEMENT CALCULATIONS

Immediate Settlement:
- Sand/gravel: typically 0-1 cm
- Stiff clay: 0-2 cm
- Soft clay: 2-8 cm

Consolidation Settlement:
Calculate using: S = (Cc * H / (1 + e0)) * log10((p0 + Δp) / p0)
Where:
- Cc = compression index
- H = layer thickness
- e0 = initial void ratio
- p0 = initial effective stress
- Δp = stress increase from loading

Total settlement should not exceed 5 cm for most structures.
Differential settlement: not exceed 1/500 of span length.

SECTION 4: FROST HEAVE PROTECTION

Regions and Frost Depths:
- Central Anatolia: 0.8-1.0m
- Marmara Region: 0.6-0.8m
- Coastal regions: 0.4-0.6m
- Mediterranean: 0.2-0.4m

Mitigation measures:
- Place foundation below frost line
- Use gravel below foundation to allow drainage
- Avoid fine-grained soils near foundation

SECTION 5: FOUNDATION TYPES

Strip Footings:
- For linear loads (walls)
- Economical for ordinary buildings
- Width typically 0.8-2.0 meters
- Depth 1.5-3.0 meters

Pad Footings:
- For isolated column loads
- Size determined by bearing capacity and column loads
- Square pads preferred for uniform settlement

Raft Foundations:
- For poor soil conditions
- When isolated footings would be too large
- Connects all columns

Piles:
- For very soft soils or deep water tables
- End-bearing or friction piles
- Depth determined by soil profile

SECTION 6: GEOTECHNICAL INVESTIGATION REQUIREMENTS

For buildings up to 5 stories: minimum 1-2 boreholes
For buildings 5-20 stories: minimum 2-4 boreholes
For special structures: detailed investigation with multiple boreholes

Minimum borehole depth:
- 10-15 meters for buildings
- Must penetrate competent bearing layer
- Must assess groundwater conditions
